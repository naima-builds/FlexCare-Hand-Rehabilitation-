How to present this model

  1. Save a sample patient profile.
  2. Choose an exercise and target angle.
  3. Open Live Session and start it.
  4. Enter the patient’s voluntary angle and watch the 3D model follow automatically; record it, assist the gap, then continue to the next repetition.
  5. Log repetitions, stop the session, and show the report / CSV export.
